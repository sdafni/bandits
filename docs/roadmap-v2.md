# SafeKey Roadmap (V2)

## V2 goals
Scale from individual landlord MVP to multi-account rental operations and partner distribution.

## Phase 1 — Workspace expansion
- Agency accounts with multiple landlord portfolios
- Property manager role with multi-property hierarchy
- Team permissions (owner, analyst, billing admin)
- Reference: `src/lib/roles.ts`

## Phase 2 — Operational depth
- Automated reference checks and document OCR improvements
- SLA timers and analyst assignment queue
- Bulk case import for agencies
- Notification layer (email/SMS) for upload and report events

## Phase 3 — Partner distribution
- Protection partner API for eligibility + quote handoff
- White-label tenant upload pages
- Revenue share reporting for agencies and partners

## Phase 4 — Market expansion
- Additional EU markets with localized document packs
- English/Greek bilingual tenant upload experience
- Regulatory disclosure templates per jurisdiction

## Not in V2 (intentionally deferred)
- Consumer tenant marketplace
- Automated lease signing
- Full insurance underwriting automation without human review

## Success metrics
- Time from case open → recommendation
- % cases with complete document packs
- Conversion from screening → protection package presentation
- MRR from subscriptions + per-case screenings
